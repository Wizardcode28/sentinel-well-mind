1. Operational Problem Statement & Deficiencies

Personnel serving in Central Armed Police Forces (CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB), the Armed Forces, and State Police formations routinely operate in hazardous environments involving prolonged remote deployments, irregular circadian rhythms, frequent night watches, and family separation. Today's welfare frameworks suffer from three severe limitations:

Latency in Identification
Stress is observed manually only after operational breakdown, behavioral decline, or critical incidents occur.
Career Stigmatization & Fear
Troops hesitate to report psychological strain due to fears of peer judgment, unfitness categorization, or adverse postings.

2. The SentinelWell Technological Approach

SentinelWell AI introduces an indigenous intelligence pipeline fusing objective duty roster telemetry with voluntary, low-friction self-check-ins:

  • Dual-Model Machine Learning Engine: Couples an unsupervised Isolation Forest (detecting abrupt schedule anomalies) with a LightGBM Gradient Boosted Classifier (classifying risk across LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, and CRITICAL bands).
  • Explainable AI via SHAP Game Theory: Eliminates algorithmic black-box opacity by extracting exact Shapley values to pinpoint the top three primary trigger stressors (e.g., 14 consecutive duty days, 8 night shifts in 30 days, < 5h sleep).
  • Support-Oriented Action Hub: Replaces punitive measures with proactive organizational solutions: mandatory 48-hour rest cycles, roster rotations, accelerated leave coordination, and voluntary confidential counseling.
